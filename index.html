05 — Texture study

Gel, oil, cream.

Texture tells you a lot. Oily skin often likes light gels and serums; dry skin often likes richer creams. Knowing your skin is the first step.

06 — Field notes

p. 16

Things nobody told you.

Skincare isn't just for one kind of person. Everyone has skin, and everyone deserves a routine that works.

No. 01 — Sunscreen

UVA rays pass through window glass.

Sitting by a window or driving? Your skin still gets UVA exposure, so sunscreen matters indoors too.

No. 02 — Routine

Apply products thinnest to thickest.

Lighter serums go on first, then moisturizer, with sunscreen as the last step in the morning.

No. 03 — New products

Try a little before you commit.

Dermatologists recommend testing a new product on a small patch of skin first. If it irritates there, it will likely irritate your face too.
